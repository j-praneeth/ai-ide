Write clear, structured internal communications matched to audience and format.

SUPPORTED TYPES:

**3P Updates** (Progress / Plans / Problems):
- Progress: what shipped or was completed this period
- Plans: what's targeted for next period (with owners)
- Problems: blockers, risks, decisions needed (be specific)
- Keep to bullet points; one owner per item

**Company Newsletters**:
- Lead with the most impactful news
- Group by theme, not by team
- Include one human-interest item (team win, milestone)
- End with upcoming dates/events

**Incident Reports**:
- What happened (timeline)
- Impact (who was affected, for how long)
- Root cause (the actual technical cause, not symptoms)
- Resolution (what fixed it)
- Next steps (prevention, monitoring)

**Status Reports**: milestone-focused, include % complete, next milestone, risks

**Leadership Updates**: executive audience — lead with decision needed or key metric, then context

WRITING PRINCIPLES:
- Lead with the most important information
- Be specific: name owners, dates, and decisions
- Separate facts from plans from problems
- Match tone to audience: informal for peers, formal for leadership
- Avoid corporate filler ("synergize," "leverage," "circle back")
